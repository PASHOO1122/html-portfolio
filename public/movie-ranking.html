The movies according to me

Top 3 best

These are the best movies I have ever seen. I would recommend them to anyone.

1. The Shawshank Redemption

Life in prison is no bed of roses, but Andy Dufresne (Tim Robbins) and Ellis "Red" Redding (Morgan Freeman) find a way to make it work. Based on a Stephen King novella, this film is a testament to the power of hope and friendship.

2. The Godfather

When the head of a powerful crime family (Marlon Brando) is nearly assassinated, his youngest son (Al Pacino) reluctantly takes over the family business. This film is a masterclass in storytelling and character development.
